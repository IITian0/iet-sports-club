Who we are

About the club

The IET Sports Club is the official sports body of the Institute of Engineering & Technology, Bundelkhand University, Jhansi. We run teams and training across nine sports, organise inter-department leagues and the annual athletics meet, and give every student a place to play, compete and represent the university.

Office bearers and sport captains are chosen each session, and the club works with the university sports office for grounds, equipment and inter-college entries.

Our mission

Make sport accessible to every student at IET — from first-time players to university-level athletes.

Facilities

Cricket & football grounds, volleyball and kabaddi court, basketball court, indoor hall for badminton and table tennis, and an athletics track.

What we run

Selection trials, inter-department leagues, inter-college tournaments, the annual athletics day and coaching clinics.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything a new player usually asks.

Who can join the IET Sports Club?

Any student currently enrolled at the Institute of Engineering & Technology, Bundelkhand University. There is no trial to become a member — trials are only for selecting the university-level teams.

Is there a membership fee?

Basic club membership is free. Some tournaments and travel may involve a small shared contribution, which is announced in advance on the notice board.

How do I get selected for a team?

Register on this site, then attend the selection trials for your sport. Trials are announced under Events. Selection is based on the trial performance and the captain's assessment.

I have never played a sport before. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Coaching sessions and beginner-friendly practice slots are available across all nine sports. Most of our members started as beginners.

Do I need my own equipment?

Basic equipment (balls, nets, rackets) is provided at the grounds. For personal gear like cricket kit or spikes, bring your own if you have it — the club helps where it can.

Can I play more than one sport?

Yes. You may register for one primary sport, but you are welcome to attend practice for others as well.
